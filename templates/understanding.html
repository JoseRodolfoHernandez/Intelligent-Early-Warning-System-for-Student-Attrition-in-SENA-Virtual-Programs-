Phase 2: Data Understanding

Dataset Selection & Lineage

The historical records are sourced directly from the Colombian Open Data Portal (datos.gov.co), specifically the official dataset cataloging the Deserción de la Formación Profesional Integral del SENA. The target pipeline isolates records where MODALIDAD_FORMACION == 'VIRTUAL' to satisfy statistical parameters (minimum 1,000 comprehensive records).

Exploratory Data Analysis & Quality Audit

  • Variable Analysis: Features include categorical dimensions (SENA Regionals, Training Levels) and numerical inputs (Total Enrolled Students).
  • Data Quality Evaluation: The initial audit detected nested double quotes (e.g., ""VIRTUAL"") and erratic trailing whitespaces that required systematic parsing.
  • Statistical Summaries: The group size distributions and timeline limits were evaluated using standard skewness boundaries to locate historical variance.
