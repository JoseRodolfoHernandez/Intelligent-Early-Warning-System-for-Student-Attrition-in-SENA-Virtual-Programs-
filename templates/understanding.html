Phase 2: Data Understanding & Statistical Audit

Dataset Source & Scope

The system utilizes the official national repository from datos.gov.co tracking "Deserción de la Formación Profesional Integral del SENA". The extracted instance contains over 15,400 active historical records, vastly exceeding the required minimum of 1000 observations, ensuring high statistical significance.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Variables

The operational features span multiple categorical and numerical distributions:

Variable NameData TypeDescription / Context

Data Quality & Correlation Assessment

  • Missing & Null Value Analysis: Core administrative tokens presented 0% global structural missing fields, but displayed critical text corruptions (nested escape sequences).
  • Anomalies Detected: High variance inside TOTAL_APRENDICES_MATRICULADOS, tracking extreme density spikes in specific technical certificates that highly correlate with immediate structural dropouts.
