Phase 1: Business Understanding

Problem Context & Description

The Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje (SENA) manages virtual learning programs nationwide. E-learning environments experience high student attrition rates due to technological barriers, asymmetric internet bandwidth, and low initial digital literacy. This creates a decoupling between the learner and the institution, causing premature abandonment of courses.

Business Objectives & Expected Impact

  • Resource Optimization: Shift from a reactive administrative posture to a proactive intervention model, allowing tutors to focus retention strategies on the top 20% highest-risk cohorts.
  • Capacity Management: Mitigate the financial and operational impact caused by mass dropouts in virtual programs, ensuring better public fund utilization.

Key Research Questions

  1. Is there a direct correlation between the scheduled duration of a course (in days) and its final attrition rate?
  2. Which SENA regionals show higher historical vulnerability to student dropouts in virtual environments?
  3. Does the group size (Total Enrolled Students) act as an operational bottleneck that triggers systemic dropouts?

Project Feasibility, Risks & Limitations

Technical Feasibility: High. Ensemble learning algorithms can efficiently handle mixed data types and high-cardinality categorical strings (like SENA regionals).

Limitations: The data relies purely on administrative macro-features. It lacks fine-grained individual student metrics (e.g., socioeconomic stratum, daily platform log frequencies) due to strict data privacy regulations.
